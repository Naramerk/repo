Console research graph: hide edge labels until hover/selection, color edges by
kind with a legend, kind checkboxes, neighbour focus dimming, «К выбранному»,
and human-readable artifact labels (id stays in details). Adds collapsible
clusters anchored on hypotheses (claim fallback, per-kind buckets) with «· N»
cards and aggregated edges, hides digest-only service artifacts behind a
«Показать служебные» toggle in the science focus, and aligns serpentine bands
so wrapped chains continue downward. Demo: `/ui/demo-graph.html`.
